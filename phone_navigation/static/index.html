Model stops · Normal mode

Works with Chill, Standard, or Assertive. Uses the driving model's predicted stop approach and imminent-stop request, as in Experimental mode, while keeping Normal cruise between stops. Does not identify every sign or light, determine right-of-way, or add friction braking. Driver accelerator overrides it; brake-paused cruise keeps its existing resume behavior. Enable while parked and disengaged.

Corner speed controls

Below 25 mph, a classified full-stalk turn requests assistance while lateral control is active. You may control gas and brake. Steering takeover cancels the turn request until the indicator is cleared. Normal road following can return when the model exits the turn. This does not specify turn radius, a 90-degree path, or prevent corner cutting. Tight low-speed turn predictions require a matching active indicator. Steering handback begins from the measured wheel angle. Default off.
